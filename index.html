课题组介绍About the Team

Research Group IntroductionResearch Group Introduction

中国科学院计算技术研究所移动互联课题组由刘敏老师领导，是国内较早从事网络互联与智能协同计算研究的团队之一。课题组牵头制订多项国际标准，负责多项重大科研项目与企业研发项目，并与移动、电信、联通、华为等公司保持长期合作。Led by Prof. Min Liu, the ICT Mobile Computing Team is among the early Chinese research teams working on network interconnection and intelligent collaborative computing. The team has contributed to international standards, major research projects, and industry collaborations, while maintaining close cooperation with China Mobile, China Telecom, China Unicom, Huawei and other companies..

课题组成果发表于 TMC、JSAC、TPDS、TVT、TIFS、TITS、TOSN、INFOCOM、IPDPS、IJCAI、AAAI、EMNLP、ACL、CIKM 等国际高水平期刊与会议，相关成果已在中国联通、联想、华为、中兴等企业的核心网络、设备产品和平台中得到应用。The team has published in leading journals and conferences such as TMC, JSAC, TPDS, TVT, TIFS, TITS, TOSN, INFOCOM, IPDPS, IJCAI, AAAI, EMNLP, ACL, and CIKM. Its research outcomes have been applied in core networks, device products, and platforms of major industry partners.

招生信息Admissions

课题组每年招收 4-5 名硕士或博士研究生，欢迎有志于前沿网络与智能系统研究的同学报考。有志于保研至本课题组的请在三到九月份联系。The team recruits 4-5 master or PhD students each year. Students interested in networked intelligence and mobile computing are welcome to apply. Students interested in pursuing postgraduate recommendation admission to our research group are encouraged to contact us between March and September.

研究方向Research Areas

围绕移动互联与智能协同计算展开Mobile interconnection and collaborative intelligence

01

网络安全与可靠通信Network Security and Reliable Communication

关注网络安全、可靠传输、研究网络攻防、漏洞挖掘与数据安全防护。 Focuses on cybersecurity, reliable transmission, network attack and defense, vulnerability discovery, and data security protection.

02

智能体Agent

关注可信安全智能体、多智能体协同、大模型智能体Focuses on trustworthy and secure agents, multi-agent collaboration, and large language model-based agents.

03

可信大模型Trustworthy Large Models

关注可信人工智能、可信大语言模型。Focuses on trustworthy artificial intelligence and trustworthy large language models.

04

移动计算与网络协同Mobile Computing and Network Collaboration

关注无人系统网络协同、移动边缘计算、工业互联网、无线网络。Focuses on unmanned-system collaboration, mobile edge computing, industrial Internet, wireless networks, and intelligent transportation systems.

05

网络安全与可靠通信Network Security and Reliable Communication

关注网络安全、可靠传输、资源受限网络中的能效通信与数据采集机制。Focuses on network security, reliable transmission, energy-efficient communication, and data collection in resource-constrained networks.
